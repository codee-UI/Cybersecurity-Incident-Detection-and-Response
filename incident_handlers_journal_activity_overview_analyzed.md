- **What:** The attackers deployed ransomware that encrypted the clinic's critical files, including medical records. Employees could not access necessary files and software, and a ransom note demanded a large sum of money in exchange for the decryption key.
- **When:** The incident occurred on a Tuesday at approximately 9:00 a.m. The scenario does not provide a specific calendar date for the incident.
- **Where:** The incident affected the computer systems and network of a small U.S. health care clinic that provides primary-care services.
- **Why:** The attackers gained access through targeted phishing emails sent to several employees. The emails contained malicious attachments that installed malware when downloaded; the ransom demand suggests that the attackers' motive was financial.
